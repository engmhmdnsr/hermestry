Hermes Mobile runs your AI assistant directly on your phone. Chats, memory, routines and the gateway itself all live on your device. Nothing routes through our servers.

HOW IT WORKS
Install once (a one-time system download of about 300 MB), add your own provider API key, and start chatting. The gateway starts with the app and keeps running in the background.

PHONE CONTROL
Ask the assistant about your phone and control it in plain words: check battery, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, set volume, adjust brightness, vibrate, toggle the flashlight, or lock the screen. Anything that needs your approval asks once in Settings, Phone, then works by request.

YOUR MODELS, YOUR KEYS
Bring your own key from your provider and switch models without restarting. Every finished reply is labeled with the model that actually served it, so you always know what answered.

ROUTINES AND MEMORY
Schedule repeated work with jobs and routines, keep long-term memory across chats, and inspect everything from the diagnostics screens: connection path test, logs, backup and restore.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your keys stay in encrypted storage on the device. Chats stay on the device. The only network calls are the ones your provider needs to answer.

REQUIREMENTS
Android 9 or newer, about 500 MB free for the on-device system, and an API key from a supported provider.
